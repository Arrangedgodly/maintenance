# Exterior & Roof Inspection

> The first home-envelope lesson. Everything here is a water-management problem following one chain: roof → gutters/downspouts → grading → foundation. Ties directly to the basement-backup origin story.

## Summary
The master model is a water chain — **roof → gutters/downspouts → [[GLOSSARY#Grading (site)|grading]] → foundation** — and a $5,000 foundation repair usually starts as a $0 drainage problem. Inspect the roof from the ground with binoculars: missing/curling shingles, [[GLOSSARY#Granule loss (shingles)|granules]] in the gutters (end-of-life sign), cracked [[GLOSSARY#Pipe boot (vent boot)|pipe boots]] (the #1 mystery leak below a bathroom), and rusted [[GLOSSARY#Roof flashing|flashing]] or debris-packed [[GLOSSARY#Roof valley|valleys]] leak more often than the shingles themselves. Start on the north and shaded sides — wear begins there.

Gutters and downspouts are the highest-leverage basement-water fix: overflow means a clog, sagging gutters mean the [[GLOSSARY#Fascia|fascia]] behind them is rotted, and downspouts discharging at the foundation are the top grading sin — extend ≥4 ft. [[GLOSSARY#Soffit|Soffit]] gaps are pest entry; blocked soffit vents cook the attic. The [[GLOSSARY#Grading (site)|grading]] check is a 6" drop over the first 10 ft sloping away; negative grade (toward the house) plus clogged gutters is what builds the [[GLOSSARY#Hydrostatic pressure|hydrostatic pressure]] that cracks foundation walls.

Read foundation cracks by orientation: vertical hairlines are normal shrinkage, but a [[GLOSSARY#Hydrostatic pressure|horizontal]] crack means the wall is being pushed in — fix drainage first, then call an engineer. [[GLOSSARY#Efflorescence|Efflorescence]] (white powder) is a cosmetic red flag for water migration — high on the wall points to surface water (gutters/grading), low points to groundwater. The hidden structural killer is the [[GLOSSARY#Deck ledger board|deck ledger]]: a soft probe at the house joint with missing [[GLOSSARY#Ledger flashing (Z-flashing)|ledger flashing]] means rot — stop using the deck. Run the twice-a-year walk-around in [[ACTION-ITEMS]] and the [[Master Seasonal Checklist|seasonal checklist]].

## The water-flow model
Every problem here is a water-management problem. Chain:
**Roof → gutters/downspouts → grading → foundation.**

Water wrong at the top rots structure from above. Water wrong at the bottom builds [[GLOSSARY#Hydrostatic pressure|hydrostatic pressure]] against the foundation — the force behind your basement backup. Fix at the top and the bottom takes care of itself.

> **"A $5,000 foundation repair usually starts as a $0 drainage problem."**

## Part 1 — Roof (inspect from GROUND — binoculars help; never climb)

| Sign | Means |
|---|---|
| Missing shingles / color change | Wind damage or end-of-life adhesive |
| Curling/cracking/cupping | Shingles aging — nearing replacement |
| Excessive [[GLOSSARY#Granule loss (shingles)|granules]] in gutters | Shingle surface sloughing — end of life |
| Cracked rubber around vent pipes ([[GLOSSARY#Pipe boot (vent boot)|pipe boots]]) | #1 cause of mystery leak below a bathroom. ~$150 pro fix |
| Rusted/lifted [[GLOSSARY#Roof flashing|flashing]] at chimney/skylights/walls | More common leak source than shingles |
| Debris/wear in [[GLOSSARY#Roof valley|valleys]] | Valleys carry most water; dams cause inside-corner leaks |
| Sagging roofline (dip from across street) | Structural — pro immediately |

> **Pro tip:** start on the **north side** and shaded areas — shingle wear and moisture damage begin there first.

### When to call a roofer vs. re-roof
- Few isolated missing/damaged shingles → repair (few hundred $)
- Widespread granule loss, curling, multiple leaks → re-roof (most asphalt roofs 20–30 yr)
- Single cracked pipe boot → ~$150 boot replacement (NOT a re-roof)

## Part 2 — Gutters, downspouts, fascia, soffit (highest-leverage for basement water)

### Gutters & downspouts
- **Overflow in rain** = clog → clean
- **Sagging gutters** = [[GLOSSARY#Fascia|fascia]] rotted behind them → press to check
- **Downspout discharge at foundation** = #1 grading problem. Extend **≥4 ft** away
- **Disconnected/crushed extensions** = check each one carries water away

### Fascia & soffit
- Soft spots / peeling paint / rot on fascia (behind gutters)
- [[GLOSSARY#Soffit|Soffit]] holes/gaps = pest entry (mice, wasps, birds)
- Blocked soffit vents (insulation pushed against from inside) → hot/humid attic

## Part 3 — Grading & foundation (the origin-story connection)

### The grading check
Soil should slope **away** from foundation: **6" drop over first 10 ft (~5% slope)**.

| ✅ Positive grade | ❌ [[GLOSSARY#Grading (site)|Negative grade]] |
|---|---|
| Slope falls away from house | Slope falls toward house |
| Water runs away from wall | Water pools at foundation → pressure → cracks |

**Causes of negative grade:** settling soil, landscaping beds against house, tilted concrete patios/walks, downspouts dumping at base.

### The fix
1. Add clean fill soil (NOT gravel — water flows through). Slope away. Afternoon + rake.
2. Keep soil ≥6" below siding (rot/termite access).
3. Extend every downspout 4+ ft from foundation.
4. Clean gutters (don't overflow + saturate soil at wall).
5. Regrading not enough (low-lying property) → French/curtain drain (pro).

### Foundation crack reading

| Type | Cause | Urgency |
|---|---|---|
| Vertical, <1/8" | Normal concrete shrinkage | Low — monitor |
| Vertical, widening or >1/4" | Settlement, ongoing movement | Medium — engineer |
| **Horizontal** (any size, esp. >1/4") | **[[GLOSSARY#Hydrostatic pressure|Hydrostatic pressure]]** pushing wall in | **High — engineer + fix drainage immediately** |
| Stair-step (block/brick mortar joints) | Differential settlement / lateral pressure | Medium — serious if widening/bowing |

> ⚠️ **Horizontal cracks are the urgent one.** Soil outside saturated → pushing wall inward. **Fix drainage first** (grading, gutters, downspouts), then engineer for wall reinforcement.

### Efflorescence ([[GLOSSARY#Efflorescence|white powder]] on basement walls)
Cosmetic itself, **red flag for water migration through foundation.**
- **Near top of wall** = surface water (grading/gutters)
- **Near bottom / floor joint** = groundwater/high water table (sump/drain tile)

Start at roof/gutters, work down. Exterior drainage fix often resolves without interior waterproofing.

### Crack monitoring
Crack gauge (Avongard tell-tale, ~$10) across any watched crack. Check monthly + after heavy rain/freeze-thaw. Movement = active failure → engineer.

## Part 4 — Siding, windows, deck ledger

### Siding
- **Vinyl:** cracks, holes, loose panels, faded/brittled
- **Wood:** peeling paint (moisture from behind), soft spots (rot), insect damage
- **Stucco:** cracks — esp. long diagonal (settlement)
- **Brick:** stair-step mortar cracking, spalling (flaking)

### Windows & doors
- Failed caulk around trim → water into wall. Re-caulk cracked/shrinking joints.
- Peeling paint above windows = water behind trim from above.
- Weatherstripping — replace compressed before winter.

### Deck ledger ([[GLOSSARY#Deck ledger board|hidden critical connection]])
Board bolted to house holding deck on. Water behind it (missing [[GLOSSARY#Ledger flashing (Z-flashing)|flashing]]) → ledger + house's band joist rot → deck separates. Leading cause of deck collapses.
- Look for flashing above ledger (tucked behind siding, lapping over top edge). **None visible = assume missing (common on older decks).**
- Probe ledger with screwdriver at house joint. **Soft/crumbly = rot.**
- Look inside (basement/crawlspace) at rim joist behind ledger — water stains/rot/insects.
- Loose/corroded lag bolts. IRC requires through-bolts/specific lag screws; older decks with nails are failure-prone.

> ⚠️ **Soft ledger = structural emergency.** Stop using deck. Call deck contractor. Repair = replace ledger + repair band joist + proper flashing.

## The twice-a-year walk-around (spring + fall, ~30 min)
- [ ] **Roof (binoculars):** missing/curling shingles, pipe boots, flashing, valleys, sagging
- [ ] **Gutters/fascia:** overflow, sag, downspout extensions 4+ ft, fascia rot, soffit holes
- [ ] **Grading/foundation:** slope away, soil below siding, crack types/widths, efflorescence, water stains
- [ ] **Walls/windows/deck:** siding cracks, caulk, deck ledger flashing + solid wood, railings, limbs over roof

## DIY / Pro boundary
| DIY (inspection + minor fixes) | Call a pro |
|---|---|
| Ground-level roof inspection | Anything on the roof surface itself |
| Gutter cleaning, downspout extensions | Fascia/soffit replacement (height work) |
| Grading fix (add soil, slope away) | Foundation crack >1/4" or horizontal/active |
| Crack gauge installation + monitoring | Deck ledger rot (structural) |
| Re-caulking windows/doors | Re-roof or flashing replacement |
| Siding minor repair | Siding replacement / stucco crack diagnosis |
