# Finding & Vetting a Pro

> Sits above every arc's "call a pro" boundary — every DIY/pro line in the workspace becomes actionable once you know how to find, vet, and hire.

## Summary
A five-step process: **SOURCE** ≥3 candidates (word of mouth and trade referrals beat online reviews), **VERIFY** the non-negotiable three — [[GLOSSARY#License (contractor)|license]] (look up on the state board website), insurance via a [[GLOSSARY#Certificate of Insurance (COI)|COI emailed by the broker]] (both [[GLOSSARY#General liability insurance|general liability]] and [[GLOSSARY#Workers' compensation insurance|workers' comp]]; without workers' comp an injured worker can sue you), and an active [[GLOSSARY#Bond (contractor's bond / surety bond)|surety bond]] — then **SOLICIT** 3 written [[GLOSSARY#Bid (quote / estimate / proposal)|bids]] on the same scope (line-itemed, not single bottom-lines), **CHECK** ≥2 references plus review patterns, and **CONTRACT** with milestone payments tied to demonstrable work. Make bids comparable by writing one detailed scope of work and giving it to every bidder; a bid 30%+ below the cluster is a red flag, not a deal. Every [[GLOSSARY#Change order|change order]] in writing before the work. Payment schedule: deposit ≤10–30%, progress payments on milestones, final 10–20% only after you're satisfied and [[GLOSSARY#Lien waiver|lien waivers]] are collected. Three absolute walk-away red flags: cash for a discount, sign-today-to-start-tomorrow pressure, and "skip the permit." This is the gate every [[Sump Pump Maintenance|sump]] / [[Electrical Safety Fundamentals|electrical]] / [[Water Heater Maintenance|water heater]] / [[Exterior & Roof Inspection|roof]] pro in the workspace passes through.

## The five-step process
1. **SOURCE** — find 3+ candidates
2. **VERIFY** — license + COI + bond (independently)
3. **SOLICIT** — 3 written bids on same scope
4. **CHECK** — references + reviews
5. **CONTRACT** — scope + milestone payments

## Step 1 — Source (by signal quality)
| Source | Why |
|---|---|
| **Word of mouth** (neighbor/friend) | Highest — real customer, real outcome. Ask: would you hire again? On time? Final price match bid? |
| **Trade referrals** | Trusted plumber knows good electrician. Pros refer pros. |
| **State licensing board database** | Search by trade + zip; actively licensed, no complaints |
| **Manufacturer dealer locators** | Brand-specific (Carrier, Bradford White, Maytag) certified installers |
| Angi/Thumbtack/Google reviews | Source of candidates only; gameable. Filter for volume (50+) + patterns, not single anecdotes |
| Craigslist/Facebook/door hangers | Lowest signal; high fraud/unlicensed rates. Extra-vigorous verification required |

**Goal:** ≥3 candidates to bid.

## Step 2 — Verify (the non-negotiable three)

### License
- Look up on **state licensing board website** (CSLB CA, L&I WA, DOS NY — search "[state] contractor license lookup")
- Active + current; classifications match project; no recent complaints/suspensions; name matches business

### Insurance — via COI from broker
Ask contractor to have **insurance broker email COI directly** (not a photocopy). Two coverages:
- **[[GLOSSARY#General liability insurance|General liability]]** — property damage from their work. Limits ≥ project value.
- **[[GLOSSARY#Workers' compensation insurance|Workers' comp]]** — injured employee on your property. **Without it, they can sue you.** Sole proprietors may be exempt — confirm exemption is real.

> ⚠️ **COI must come from broker, not contractor.** Photocopy can be expired/forged. Broker email on letterhead = verification.

### Bond
[[GLOSSARY#Bond (contractor's bond / surety bond)|Surety bond]] = financial backstop if they abandon/shoddy/unpaid subs. Verify active on licensing board site alongside license. Typically $5k–$25k. Separate from insurance.

## Step 3 — Solicit 3 written bids on same scope

### Make bids comparable
1. **Write a scope of work** — same for each bidder. Detailed, not vague ("replace water heater" → specify model, gas flex, T&P pipe, permit, haul-away).
2. **Specify materials** — brand/model/capacity/efficiency.
3. **Specify permit responsibility** — contractor or homeowner. $200–600 line item.
4. **Specify cleanup + haul-away.**
5. **Ask line-item breakdown** — labor/materials/permit/disposal. Single bottom-line tells nothing.

### Reading wildly different bids
$2,500 / $2,700 / $4,500 → the $4,500 may be bidding different scope (better materials, permit included). Compare **line by line**. The $2,500 may have omitted permit/disposal/gas flex.

> ⚠️ **Suspiciously low bid (30%+ below cluster) = red flag, not a deal.** Usually: missed scope / inferior materials / unlicensed uninsured / change-order bait-and-switch.

> **Don't reveal other bids.** "What did others quote?" → don't answer. Common manipulation: undercut by $100.

## Step 4 — Check references + reviews
**Call ≥2 references. Ask:**
- Show up on time + communicate? (Most complaints are communication, not workmanship.)
- Final price match bid? (Different — why?)
- **Would you hire them again?** (Best single question.)
- Subcontractors managed?
- Cleanup?

**Reviews:** patterns across many (4.2 avg/200 reviews > 5.0/6). Filter for project similar to yours.

## Step 5 — Contract + payment

### Contract must include
- Scope of work (same as bid)
- Total price + payment schedule
- Materials list (brand/model)
- Start/completion dates
- Permit responsibility
- Workmanship warranty (separate from material warranties)
- Change order clause — all in writing, signed, before work

### Payment schedule — milestone, never large upfront
| Pay | Why / red flag |
|---|---|
| **Deposit ≤10–30%** or materials cost (state law caps vary; CA: 10% or $1k) | 50%+ upfront = funding another project with your money |
| **Progress payments tied to milestones** | "30% rough-in, 30% drywall, 30% inspection, 10% punch-list." Pay as work demonstrably done. |
| **Final 10–20% only after satisfied + [[GLOSSARY#Lien waiver|lien waivers]] collected** | Final payment is leverage for punch list. Waivers prevent subcontractor liens. |

> ⚠️ **Three absolute red flags — walk away:**
> 1. **"Cash for a discount"** — no paper trail; tax evasion/fraud. Licensed pros pay taxes.
> 2. **"Sign today to start tomorrow"** — high-pressure prevents verification. Good pro doesn't fear your checks.
> 3. **"Skip the permit, it'll slow us down"** — illegal, uninspected, surfaces at resale. Shifting liability to you.

| Need / trade | License class to verify |
|---|---|
| HVAC tune-up / refrigerant | HVAC contractor — HVAC (C-20 CA) + EPA refrigerant cert |
