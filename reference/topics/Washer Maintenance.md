# Washer Maintenance

> Final note of the appliance arc. Front-loaders trap moisture in an airtight drum — add warm temps, detergent residue, and a dark interior and you get a mildew factory. Fully preventable; failure mode is **odor, not danger** (contrast the [[Dryer Maintenance|dryer's]] fire risk). Unit-specific: Maytag MHW5630HC (4.5 cu.ft. front-load).

## Summary
- Maintenance = three things: (1) **get the water out** (leave the door open, wipe the gasket), (2) **stop feeding the mildew** (HE detergent, correct — usually smaller — amount), (3) **flush the system periodically** (Clean Washer cycle, pump filter).
- **HE detergent, less than you think:** HE-labeled only (non-HE suds the machine can't rinse → residue → odor), and most people overdose — follow the load-size chart on *your* bottle (concentration varies 2X/4X/8X), which for a normal front-load load is usually **~1–2 tablespoons**, not the cap fill line.
- **Leave the door cracked** after every load (~overnight). Two seconds; prevents most odor. Don't latch closed between loads.
- **Wipe the [[GLOSSARY#Door boot / gasket (washer)|door boot/gasket]] weekly** — its folds trap water, hair, lint, coins and are the #1 mildew spot. Black specks = early mildew → dilute bleach (1:10) now. Established mildew permanently stains rubber; prevention >> cure.
- **Monthly:** clean the dispenser drawer (crust → bacteria + clogged ports) and run the [[GLOSSARY#Clean Washer cycle|Clean Washer cycle]] — hot, high-water, no-clothes flush of drum/gasket/passages. **1 affresh® tablet directly in the drum** (not the dispenser; no detergent for this cycle).
- **Tier 2 — [[GLOSSARY#Pump filter (drain filter)|pump filter]] clean (annual or on symptoms):** the MHW5630HC has **no external access door** — the filter sits behind the front panels, which makes this more involved. Defer unless symptoms appear (won't drain, soaking-wet spin, "F9 E1" drain error, pump humming) or fold it into a pro appliance-service visit.
- Symptoms of a clogged pump filter: won't drain / slow drain, spin leaves clothes soaking wet, "F9 E1" or similar drain-error code, pump humming during the drain phase.
- If you do open it: unplug + shut off hot/cold supplys, **shallow pan + towels ready** (a gallon+ spills when the pump housing opens), remove top + front panels per a model-specific video, slowly twist the filter cap counterclockwise to drain before fully removing.
- Mildew is the failure mode; the [[Dryer Maintenance|dryer]] is the fire risk. Also check the [[Supply Lines, Hoses & Shut-off Valves|washer supply hoses]] — a rubber hose burst is the catastrophic counterpart to this routine-maintenance note.

## The core idea
Front-loaders trap moisture (airtight door seal holds water during cycle, traps residual moisture between cycles). Add warm temps + detergent residue + dark drum = mildew factory. Maintenance = 3 things:
1. **Get the water out** (leave door open, wipe gasket)
2. **Stop feeding the mildew** (HE detergent, correct amount)
3. **Flush the system periodically** (Clean Washer cycle, pump filter)

Failure mode is **odor/mildew** (annoying, not dangerous like the dryer's fire risk). Fully preventable.

## Tier 1 — Habits + homeowner scope

### 1. HE detergent — less than you think
- **HE-labeled detergent only.** Non-HE → excess suds machine can't rinse → residue → odor.
- **Most people overdose.** Modern HE is concentrated (2X/4X/8X) — use the bottle's load-size chart, not the cap fill line (that's calibrated for worst-case heavy soil). A normal front-load load is usually ~1–2 tablespoons.
- Over-dosing causes same sudsing/odor problems as non-HE.

### 2. Leave the door open — every load
After load finishes, **leave door cracked** (even 1") until interior dries (~overnight). 2-second habit, prevents most odor. Don't latch closed between loads.

### 3. Wipe the door boot/gasket — weekly
[[GLOSSARY#Door boot / gasket (washer)|Gasket]] folds trap water, hair, lint, coins — #1 mildew spot.
1. Cloth around inside of gasket, pull back folds.
2. Check lowest point (water pools there; look for trapped items + small drain hole that can clog).
3. Black specks (early mildew) → dilute bleach (1:10) or mildew remover, then rinse damp.

> ⚠️ **Don't neglect for months.** Established mildew permanently stains rubber — kill the organism, can't remove the stain. Prevention >> cure.

### 4. Clean dispenser drawer — monthly
Dried detergent/softener crust → bacteria/mildew + clogs dispenser ports.
1. Release drawer (push release latch inside drawer opening at back, pull fully out).
2. Disassemble inserts (detergent cup, softener cup — usually unlatch).
3. Warm soapy water + old toothbrush for crust.
4. Rinse, dry, reassemble, reinsert. Wipe the cavity too.

### 5. Clean Washer cycle — monthly
[[GLOSSARY#Clean Washer cycle|Clean Washer cycle]] = hot, high-water, no-clothes flush of drum/gasket/passages. Machine auto-prompts ~every 30 loads.
1. Drum empty (no clothes).
2. **1 affresh® tablet directly in bottom of drum** — NOT in dispenser.
3. Close door, select Clean Washer, start.
4. Leave door open after to dry.

> ⚠️ **Don't add detergent to dispenser for Clean Washer cycle.** affresh in drum only. Detergent → suds overflow through vent (cycle uses less water, can't rinse).
> No affresh? Substitute ⅔ cup liquid chlorine bleach in the bleach compartment.

## Tier 2 — Pump filter clean (annual or as needed)
> ⚠️ **The seam.** Unlike some front-loaders with an external access door, the MHW5630HC's [[GLOSSARY#Pump filter (drain filter)|pump filter]] is **behind the front panels** — requires panel removal. Tier 2: DIY-scope with basic tools, but more involved. Defer unless symptoms appear, or fold into annual appliance-service call.

### Symptoms of a clogged pump filter
- Won't drain / drains slowly
- Spin leaves clothes soaking wet
- "F9 E1" or similar drain-error code
- Humming from drain pump during drain phase

### Access reality on your model
**MHW5630HC has NO external access door.** Filter is part of drain pump at base of cabinet; reaching it = remove top + front panels. This is why it's Tier 2.

### Procedure (if you decide to do it)
Watch model-specific video first: [MHW5630HC2 drain pump filter cleanout](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a4dyOBB_mUE) · [Maytag pump-filter product help](https://producthelp.maytag.com/Laundry/Washers/Product_Info/Washer_Cleaning_and_Care/Cleaning_the_Pump_Filter_-_Front_Load_Washer).

1. Unplug washer. Shut off hot + cold supply valves.
2. **Shallow pan + towels ready** — gallon+ of water spills when pump housing opens.
3. Remove top panel (screws at back-top, slide back + lift off).
4. Remove front panel per model-specific video (screws vary by revision).
5. Locate drain pump (lower front). Twist-out filter cap.
6. **Slowly twist filter cap counterclockwise** — water drains. Let empty into pan before fully removing filter.
7. Pull filter, clean under running water (coins, hair, lint, debris).
8. Reinsert, hand-tighten cap. Reassemble panels reverse.
9. Restore water + power. Short cycle, confirm normal drain.

> **If a small drain hose sits next to the filter cap** (some revisions): pull it out, uncap, drain pump housing through the hose *before* removing filter — far less messy. Cap + re-stow, then remove filter.

> Not comfortable with panel removal? Reasonable to fold into a pro appliance-service visit — 15-min job for a tech with the machine open.

## Cadence — your unit
| Frequency | Task |
|---|---|
| Every load | Door cracked open to dry; HE detergent 1–2 tbsp |
| Weekly | Wipe gasket folds, check for trapped items |
| Monthly | Clean Washer cycle (affresh tablet); clean dispenser drawer |
| As needed / annual | Pump filter clean (Tier 2) — if draining slows or spin leaves clothes wet |
