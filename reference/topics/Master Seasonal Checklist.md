# Master Seasonal Checklist

> The integration lesson that stitches every arc — HVAC, plumbing, appliances, electrical, envelope — into one calendar of monthly, seasonal, and daylight-saving tasks.

## Summary
Three cadences run the home: a **monthly** sweep (~10 min, first Saturday) that catches the things that fail silently — the [[GLOSSARY#Air filter|HVAC filter]], [[GLOSSARY#Water meter leak indicator|water-meter leak test]], dryer lint, washer [[GLOSSARY#Door boot / gasket (washer)|gasket]], and a press-TEST on every [[GLOSSARY#CO alarm|smoke/CO detector]]; **seasonal** sweeps (spring/summer/fall/winter) that prep the home for the load it's about to face; and **daylight-saving** biannual triggers for detector batteries and fire extinguishers.

Spring wakes the cooling side (condenser, [[GLOSSARY#Condensate drain / drain line|condensate drain]], gutters). Fall is the heaviest sweep — winterization dominates: disconnect hoses, drain [[GLOSSARY#Frost-free sillcock (freeze-proof faucet)|hose bibbs]], blow out [[GLOSSARY#Sprinkler blowout|sprinklers]], insulate pipes, reverse ceiling fans, and clean gutters before freeze. Summer is monitoring (AC under load, [[GLOSSARY#Grading (site)|grading]] slope, basement humidity). Winter is damage watch — open cabinet doors in deep freezes, watch for [[GLOSSARY#Ice dam|ice dams]], know the [[GLOSSARY#Main shut-off valve|main shut-off]].

The annual deep tasks: flush the water heater and inspect the [[GLOSSARY#Anode rod (sacrificial anode)|anode rod]], clean the dryer vent end-to-end, and test every [[GLOSSARY#GFCI (Ground Fault Circuit Interrupter)|GFCI]] plus its downstream. ## Full procedure

## The cadence: three layers
- **Monthly (~10 min):** quick recurring checks that catch problems early
- **Seasonal (×4):** bigger sweeps tied to spring/summer/fall/winter
- **Daylight-saving cue (×2):** biannual tasks tied to the clock change (detector batteries)

## Monthly backbone (set a recurring reminder — first Saturday)
- [ ] Inspect HVAC air filter — replace on dirt ([[HVAC Filter Change|HVAC filter]])
- [ ] Check supply & return vents unobstructed
- [ ] Run water-meter leak test ([[Finding Leaks Early|leak test]])
- [ ] Visual sweep: under sinks, around toilets, water heater floor
- [ ] Clean dryer lint screen deeply (soapy water) ([[Dryer Maintenance|dryer]])
- [ ] Wipe washer gasket; leave door cracked ([[Washer Maintenance|washer]])
- [ ] Press TEST on every smoke & CO detector
- [ ] Test garage door auto-reverse (2×4 flat on floor + photo-eye beam break) ([[Garage Door Maintenance|garage door]])
- [ ] Glance at breaker panel — anything tripped?

## Spring (Mar–May) — wake the home up
### HVAC cooling prep
- [ ] Replace air filter; flush condensate drain (vinegar)
- [ ] Clear debris 2 ft around condenser; hose off fins; straighten bent fins
- [ ] Confirm pad level; switch thermostat to COOL; verify cold air
- [ ] Book spring pro tune-up (cooling side)
### Exterior
- [ ] Clean gutters + downspouts (winter debris)
- [ ] Confirm downspout extensions ≥4 ft from foundation
- [ ] Inspect roof from ground; check eaves for ice-dam damage
- [ ] Check hose bibbs for freeze damage before turning on
- [ ] Inspect window screens
### Plumbing & appliances
- [ ] Exercise main shut-off + fixture valves ([[Supply Lines, Hoses & Shut-off Valves|valves]])
- [ ] Check washing machine hoses — braided, no bulges
- [ ] Clean dishwasher filter; run hot empty cycle
### "Spring forward"
- [ ] Replace smoke + CO detector batteries
- [ ] Check detector age (replace at 10 yr)
- [ ] Check fire extinguisher pressure + expiration

## Summer (Jun–Aug) — monitoring + outdoor projects
- [ ] Mid-summer condensate drain check (AC under load)
- [ ] Trim plants 2 ft back from condenser
- [ ] Watch for warm air, short cycling, ice on refrigerant lines
- [ ] Check gutters after storms; inspect deck for rot/ledger flashing
- [ ] Look for wasp nests; check trees for dead limbs over roof
- [ ] Dryer vent airflow check at exterior cap
- [ ] Refrigerator: vacuum condenser coils (heat = efficiency hit)
- [ ] Run Clean Washer cycle (affresh)
- [ ] Confirm yard grading slopes away from foundation
- [ ] Check basement humidity — dehumidify if >60%

## Fall (Sep–Nov) — buttoning up (most important sweep)
### HVAC heating prep
- [ ] Replace air filter; switch to HEAT; verify warm air
- [ ] Book fall pro tune-up — gas furnace combustion + heat exchanger
- [ ] Replace furnace humidifier pad if equipped
### Winterization (critical)
- [ ] Disconnect + drain outdoor hoses; store inside
- [ ] Shut off interior hose-bibb valves; open exterior faucet to drain
- [ ] Service/blow out sprinkler system
- [ ] Insulate exposed pipes in unheated spaces
- [ ] Seal air leaks around windows + doors
- [ ] Reverse ceiling fans (clockwise, low speed)
### Exterior — water management
- [ ] **Clean gutters + downspouts thoroughly** after leaves fall, before freeze
- [ ] Confirm downspouts ≥4 ft from foundation
- [ ] Check foundation cracks; monitor season to season
- [ ] Inspect roof + flashing before snow
- [ ] Store patio furniture; cover AC condenser if manufacturer recommends
- [ ] Lubricate garage door (hinges, rollers, track, opener drive — lithium or silicone spray; never WD-40); run a balance test ([[Garage Door Maintenance|garage door]])
### "Fall back"
- [ ] Replace smoke + CO detector batteries (2nd time)
- [ ] Replace any detector >10 yr (date on back)
- [ ] Test fireplace/chimney before first use

## Winter (Dec–Feb) — damage watch
- [ ] Keep cabinet doors under sinks open during deep freezes
- [ ] Drip a faucet on coldest nights if pipes vulnerable
- [ ] Watch for ice dams (icicles/ice at eaves = heat loss + poor vent)
- [ ] Keep roof-melt socks ready
- [ ] Know where main water shut-off is — be ready to use it
- [ ] Replace air filter monthly during heavy heating
- [ ] Keep furnace intake/exhaust vents clear of snow
- [ ] If CO detector alarms: leave, call 911/gas utility. Don't investigate first.
- [ ] Check attic for frost/condensation (poor ventilation sign)
- [ ] Monitor humidity (condensation on windows = too high or cold windows)
- [ ] Inspect basement for water after thaws

## Annual deep tasks
- [ ] **Water heater:** flush tank; test T&P; inspect anode rod (outlet-nipple method, Bradford White) ([[Water Heater Maintenance|water heater]])
- [ ] **Dryer:** full vent-run clean to exterior; interior deep clean or pro service ([[Dryer Maintenance|dryer]])
- [ ] **Electrical:** exercise every fixture valve; test every GFCI + verify downstream trip ([[Outlet & GFCI Replacement|GFCI test]])
- [ ] **Records:** update maintenance log; review insurance; confirm service-line coverage rider ([[Drain & Sewer Clog Prevention|drain & sewer]])

## The why behind the cadence
- **Monthly tasks** are the ones that fail silently (filters, leaks, lint).
- **Seasonal tasks** prep the home for the load it's about to face (cooling, heating, freeze, storms).
- **Daylight-saving tasks** are the safety devices — detectors, extinguishers — that need reliable biannual triggers.
- **Fall is the heaviest sweep** because winter is unforgiving: water that freezes expands, breaks things, and causes the failures that cost the most.
