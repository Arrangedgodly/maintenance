# Dishwasher Maintenance

> A self-cleaning appliance that isn't — the food goes somewhere.

## Summary
First know which filter system you have: a [[GLOSSARY#Manual filter (dishwasher)|manual filter]] (removable mesh — Bosch, Miele, Samsung, LG, most modern; clean every 2–4 weeks) or a [[GLOSSARY#Hard food disposer (dishwasher)|hard food disposer]] (built-in grinder — GE "Piranha", some KitchenAid/Maytag; no filter maintenance). A clogged manual filter is the #1 cause of "doesn't clean well anymore" — it recirculates dirty water. Monthly, clear clogged [[GLOSSARY#Spray arms (wash arms)|spray arm]] jets with a toothpick (clogs kill top-rack cleaning) and run a cleaning cycle — use [[GLOSSARY#Citric acid clean|citric acid]] for hard water (it's the only thing that removes scale from the heater and spray jets). [[GLOSSARY#Rinse aid (rinse agent)|Rinse aid]] is not optional on modern machines (esp. Bosch/Miele with no heated-dry element) — it sheets water off for drying. Confirm the drain hose has a code-required [[GLOSSARY#High loop (drain hose)|high loop]] or it siphons sink water back in (recurring odor); if it drains into the [[Garbage Disposer|disposer]], run the disposer first. The counterintuitive rule: don't fully pre-rinse — modern detergent enzymes need food soil to activate, and pre-rinsing etches glass. Scrape solids, leave residue.

## First: know which filter system you have
| | [[GLOSSARY#Manual filter (dishwasher)|Manual filter]] | [[GLOSSARY#Hard food disposer (dishwasher)|Hard food disposer]] |
|---|---|---|
| How | Removable mesh at tub bottom | Built-in grinder pulverizes food |
| Brands | Bosch, Miele, Samsung, LG, most modern | GE "Piranha", some KitchenAid/Maytag |
| Maintenance | **Clean every 2–4 weeks** | None — no manual filter |

> Most modern machines (incl. your likely Maytag pair) use manual filters — chosen for quietness. Look for a twist-off cylinder below the bottom spray arm.

> ⚠️ **Clogged filter = #1 cause of "doesn't clean well anymore."** Recirculates dirty water, redeposits food. Highest-leverage maintenance.

## Part 1 — Clean the filter (manual-filter only)
1. Remove bottom rack.
2. Twist off filter cylinder at tub bottom (¼-turn CCW, lift). Separate upper mesh cup if present.
3. Rinse under hot water. Scrub with soft brush/old toothbrush — remove greasy film + food.
4. Check the well it sat in — scoop debris/glass fragments.
5. Reinstall — seat + lock. Unseated = unfiltered water through pump.

Cadence: every 2–4 wk. Heavy pre-rinse → stretch it. No pre-rinse (modern detergent works better) → more often.

## Part 2 — Clear spray arms (monthly)
[[GLOSSARY#Spray arms (wash arms)|Spray arms]]' jet holes clog with food + mineral scale → top rack stops cleaning (lower arm still sprays).
1. Remove arms (twist-off or clip at hub; no tools).
2. Rinse warm water, both directions.
3. Clear clogged holes with toothpick (Bosch's official method) or thin wire. Don't enlarge.
4. Spin-test by hand before reinstalling. Seized = stripped hub/warped arm → replace.
5. Reinstall. Confirm each spins freely with bottom rack in.

## Part 3 — Monthly cleaning cycle
Grease + detergent residue coat interior, heater, passages.
1. Empty (no dishes, no detergent in dispenser).
2. Cleaner choice:
   - **Citric acid powder** (~$5, 2–3 tbsp loose in tub) — **best for hard water**; dissolves mineral scale
   - Dishwasher cleaner tablet (affresh, Finish)
   - White vinegar (2 cups in bowl on bottom rack — not loose; some manufacturers advise against regular vinegar use, so citric acid is the safer periodic cleaner)
3. Hottest cycle (Heavy / Pots & Pans / dedicated Clean cycle).
4. Leave door cracked after.

> Hard water (white film on glass, crust on showerhead)? Citric acid — it's the one that actually removes scale from heater + spray-arm jets. Every 1–2 mo.

## Part 4 — Door gasket + drain

### Door gasket
Rubber seal folds trap water/slime/mildew → odor + door leaks.
- Wipe entire gasket with damp cloth, pulling open each fold.
- Black mildew → dilute bleach 1:10 or mildew remover, then damp-wipe residue.
- Check inside face of door panel below gasket — greasy film most people miss.

### Drain + high loop
- **Drain sump:** remove lower spray arm + filter, check for food/glass/utensil. Anything there restricts draining + smells.
- **[[GLOSSARY#High loop (drain hose)|High loop]]:** confirm drain hose loops up high under counter before connecting to drain/disposer. Required by code; missing = sink water siphons back into dishwasher = recurring odor.

> ⚠️ If drains into garbage disposer: run disposer empty before starting dishwasher (clogged disposer backs up into DW). Confirm knockout plug removed from disposer's DW port at install (common install error — DW can't drain at all).

## Part 5 — Rinse aid + detergent

### Rinse aid — NOT optional
[[GLOSSARY#Rinse aid (rinse agent)|Rinse aid]] sheets water off dishes so they dry by residual heat. Modern machines (esp. Bosch/Miele) have no heated-dry element — without rinse aid, dishes wet, glass spots, "doesn't dry" complaints.
- Fill dispenser in door. Lasts 1–3 mo.
- Adjustable setting → start middle, adjust on drying results.

### Detergent
- **Pods:** convenient, pre-measured, can't adjust. Don't put in dispenser if cycle has pre-wash (dissolves all at once). Some include rinse aid — don't double-fill.
- **Powder:** most flexible, best for hard water (dose higher). Often best value.
- **Gel:** weakest cleaner. Avoid for hard water.

## Part 6 — The pre-rinse myth (counterintuitive)
Modern detergents have **enzymes that need food soil to activate.** Fully pre-rinsed dishes → enzymes underperform → etched glass + residue.
- **Scrape the solids. Don't rinse the residue.** Opposite of what most were taught 20 years ago.
- Hard food disposer → handles more food. Manual filter → large chunks end up in filter (fine — that's what it's for — but clean more often).

## Cadence
| Frequency | Task |
|---|---|
| Each load | Scrape solids, don't fully rinse; leave door cracked after |
| Every 2–4 wk | Clean filter (manual-filter machines) |
| Monthly | Clear spray arms; cleaning cycle (citric acid/tablet/vinegar); wipe gasket + door; check drain sump; refill rinse aid |
| Once / diagnose | Confirm high loop; if drains to disposer: port clear; hard water → use citric acid |
